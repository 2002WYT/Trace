WORD TRACE · OFFLINE EDITION

HOW TO USE
1. Double-click "word-trace-offline-en.html".
2. Open it with a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Choose the word length and one or more exam dictionaries.
4. Type a whole guessed word, or click any tile to enter one letter.
5. New letters start gray. Click a filled tile to cycle through gray, yellow, and green.
6. Click "Find remaining words" to view every matching candidate.

NOTES
- No installation or internet connection is required.
- The complete dictionary, design, and program are embedded in the HTML file.
- All searching and filtering happen locally in your browser.
- Nothing you enter is uploaded, and no database is used.
